AI-LENS™: A Common Observation Framework for WEB-OM, WEB-AI and WEB-A2A

AI-LENS organizes observation along three dimensions: access path × category × intervention layer.

Access path

WEB-OM follows access by people. WEB-AI follows access and representation through AI systems. WEB-A2A follows operational interaction between agents.

Category

SEO, AEO, GEO, AIO, AI Signals and A2A signals have different objectives. Each category keeps its own taxonomy.

Intervention layer

ON-SITE and ON-PAGE are directly controllable by the website owner. OFF-PAGE depends on external sources and must not be presented as controllable simply by changing a site or an edge service.

Object scale

PAGE → SERVICE → COMPANY → ACTIVITY → INDUSTRY → HISTORY. Differences between models are data: they show where a representation is robust and where it depends on a system’s particular retrieval or interpretation mechanism.
