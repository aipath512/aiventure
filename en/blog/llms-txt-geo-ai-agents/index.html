How to Configure llms.txt for GEO and AI Agent Access

llms.txt can serve as a machine-readable resource describing a site’s positioning, important pages and interpretation rules. It does not guarantee indexing or citation by every AI system.

What it may contain

Canonical identity, company description, services, key pages, negative scope, governance rules, machine-readable resources, contact details and evidence sources.

The critical principle

The file must reflect the live site. An outdated llms.txt can introduce contradictions.

Verification

STATUS → CONTENT-TYPE → FINAL URL → CONTENT → REFERENCES → CURRENTNESS. Its value for GEO is to reduce ambiguity and point to canonical sources. LLMS.TXT EXISTS ≠ AI INDEXING ≠ AI CITATION.
