How to Check Whether robots.txt Works Correctly for Google and AI Crawlers

robots.txt declares access rules for crawlers; it does not prove indexing or citation.

Keep these levels separate

1. The rule is published. 2. The crawler can technically reach the resource. 3. The provider respects the rule for that crawler. 4. The content reaches an index or retrieval system. 5. The content is used or cited.

What to inspect

HTTP status, syntax, User-agent, Allow/Disallow, sitemap, redirects, WAF/Bot Management, rate limits, challenges, logs where available and crawler identity. An Allow: / line proves the published configuration, not crawling, indexing or citation. ROBOTS DECLARED ≠ CRAWLED ≠ INDEXED ≠ RETRIEVED ≠ CITED.
