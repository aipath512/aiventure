AiVenture’s Mission: From the Human Web to AI Web and A2A Web

AiVenture’s mission is to make a company’s services easy for people and AI agents to find, understand, verify and purchase—while preserving human control.

WEB-OM

A company must be discoverable and usable by people. BUSINESS → AUDIENCE → INTENT → CHANNEL → CONTENT → SERVICE → EVIDENCE → CTA → CONVERSION. WEB-OM = PULL + PUSH. People look for companies; companies reach out to people.

WEB-AI

AI systems must be able to reconstruct the company and its services accurately. AEO, GEO, AIO, AI-SEO and AI Signals build on the existing web and SEO foundation.

WEB-A2A

Services become accessible to AI agents: BUYER → BROKER → SELLER → HUMAN APPROVAL → EVIDENCE. The business defines its offer; the edge system never invents it during a transaction.

Verification

SIGNAL → OBJECT → REQUIREMENT → SOURCE → METHOD → EVIDENCE → CONTEXT → VERDICT → REMEDIATION. Possible verdicts are PASS, PARTIAL, FAIL, UNKNOWN and N/A. UNKNOWN is not FAIL.
