The AI-LENS™ Methodology: A Common Observation Framework for Human Web, AI Web and A2A Web

AI-LENS™ can provide a shared observation framework for the three ways businesses are accessed in the digital economy: WEB-OM, WEB-AI and WEB-A2A.

WEB-OM

WEB-OM describes the direct relationship between businesses and people. PULL means a person seeks out the business; PUSH means the business reaches out to a person.

WEB-AI

WEB-AI describes relationships in which AI systems mediate discovery, understanding, comparison or recommendation. The operational unit is intent: VERB/ACTION + TARGET.

WEB-A2A

WEB-A2A describes interactions between agents: BUYER ↔ BROKER ↔ SELLER, with mandates, rules, human oversight and evidence.

A common observation principle

For each access path, relevant objects, signals and criteria are defined. The same criteria are then evaluated independently with ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and Perplexity. This does not mean all categories share one signal list: SEO, AEO, GEO, AIO and AI Signals have their own versioned registries.

Three dimensions

1. Access path: WEB-OM / WEB-AI / WEB-A2A. 2. Category: SEO / AEO / GEO / AIO / AI Signals / transactional signals. 3. Intervention layer: ON-SITE / ON-PAGE / OFF-PAGE. CATEGORY × SIGNAL × OBJECT × MODEL × TIME × VERSION → OBSERVATION + EVIDENCE + VERDICT.
