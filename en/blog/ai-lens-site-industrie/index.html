AI-LENS™: From Website Analysis to a Multi-Model View of an Industry

AI-LENS™ starts from a simple idea: different AI systems may represent the same business differently. A single observation is not enough to describe that reality.

From a page to a representation of the company

The analysis begins with the homepage and continues through relevant public pages found in the sitemap. For each model, we observe what it can find, how it describes the business, which services it identifies, which sources it uses, what it omits and where contradictions arise. ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and Perplexity are treated as independent observers. Their raw responses and evidence are preserved before results are combined.

Two distinct experiments

SAME INPUT gives the same content and evidence to all models and measures differences in interpretation. NATIVE ACCESS lets each system use its own search, browsing or retrieval mechanism, measuring what it can find and represent under real operating conditions. The two experiments must not be merged into a single result.

From company to industry

The same method can be applied to businesses grouped by activity, primary or secondary CAEN code and services actually offered. Repetition over time creates a history and reveals changes within the measured sample.

The rule of truth

Agreement among four models does not replace facts verified against an authoritative source. Consensus is an observation about the models, not sufficient evidence about reality. PAGE → SERVICE → COMPANY → ACTIVITY → INDUSTRY → HISTORY.
