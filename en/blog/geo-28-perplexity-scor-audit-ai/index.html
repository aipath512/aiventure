GEO 28 × Perplexity: How a GEO Score Is Calculated and Verified

A GEO audit must distinguish the technical score of a page from what any individual AI system observes.

PASS coverage

The number of PASS signals measures coverage, not necessarily weighted quality.

Weighted GEO score

GEO = Σ(sᵢ × wᵢ) / Σ(wᵢ) for evaluable signals. N/A is excluded. UNKNOWN is not FAIL.

Perplexity observation

Perplexity is an external observer: what it finds, which sources it uses, what it cites and omits. GEO SCORE ≠ PERPLEXITY SCORE. GEO PASS COVERAGE, GEO WEIGHTED SCORE, MODEL OBSERVABILITY, MODEL AGREEMENT and EVIDENCE COVERAGE must be recorded separately. Any list of 28 signals must be tied to an explicit registry version.
