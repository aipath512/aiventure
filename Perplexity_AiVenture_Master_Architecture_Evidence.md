## 3. Certified Principles

1. **The service is the unit of value.**
   The service owns the methodology, expertise, scope, deliverables, commercial terms, price, accountability, and intellectual property.

2. **The agent is not the offer.**
   The agent makes a declared service agent-accessible and, where possible, agent-executable. It may execute, assist, orchestrate, or monitor within declared boundaries.

3. **A2A is not the service.**
   A2A is the machine-to-machine door through which authorized agents and systems may discover, evaluate, request, and consume a service.

4. **HITL remains a governance layer.**
   Human approval, override, exception handling, escalation, and accountability apply wherever risk, judgment, commercial commitment, legal implications, or material impact require them.

5. **RRVI™ is the transversal evidence layer.**
   RRVI™ supplies provenance, verification, confidence, limitations, timestamps, and traceability for claims, results, and agent actions.

6. **The offer is defined before a transaction.**
   The service owner declares the offer, buyer eligibility, required inputs, permitted outputs, scope, exclusions, price, SLA, control points, and evidence requirements before agentic interaction begins.

7. **THE EDGE MUST NEVER INVENT THE OFFER DURING A TRANSACTION.**
   No web interface, copilot, agent, workflow, or A2A endpoint may invent an undeclared service, scope, price, SLA, guarantee, condition, exclusion, or commercial obligation during a transaction.

## 4. Certified Service Surfaces

| Door | Primary consumer | Function |
|---|---|---|
| Human Web | Client, founder, manager, specialist, B2B buyer | Discover, understand, request, approve, and buy the service |
| Web-AI / Copilot | Human assisted by AI | Clarify needs, structure a mandate, interpret options, prepare decisions |
| Web-A2A | Buyer Agent, Seller Agent, Broker Agent, procurement system | Submit structured mandates and receive structured results, evidence, limitations, and next actions |

The underlying service remains consistent across all three doors. Access method and permissible automation may differ; service identity and commercial boundaries do not.

---

## 5. Certified Agent Roles

Depending on service design, risk level, data availability, and declared operating rules, an agent may:

- Execute bounded and low-risk activities.
- Assist human specialists with analysis, research, structuring, drafts, and checks.
- Orchestrate tools, data sources, specialist actions, and other agents.
- Monitor signals, changes, exceptions, performance, and compliance conditions.
- Prepare outcomes for mandatory human approval.
- Return outputs with evidence, confidence, provenance, and limitations.

The official formulation is:

> The offer remains yours. The methodology remains yours. The agent makes it agent-accessible and, where possible, agent-executable. A2A opens the machine-to-machine channel. HITL preserves control. RRVI™ provides evidence.
