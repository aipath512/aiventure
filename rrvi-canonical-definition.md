# RRVI™ — Canonical Definition

## Canonical name
RRVI™ — Regulation, Rules, Verification and Intervention.

## Canonical meaning
RRVI is AiVenture’s pre-action verification gate for regulation, jurisdiction, permissions, autonomy and approvals before a sensitive action is executed by a human, AI system or Agent.

## Operational purpose
RRVI determines whether an action may proceed, must be limited, or must be escalated for human approval. It preserves evidence of the applicable rule, jurisdiction, permissions, autonomy boundary, approval, actor, decision and result.

## Decision states
- ALLOW: the action satisfies the applicable rules and permissions.
- LIMIT: the action may proceed only within explicit constraints.
- ESCALATE: a human or authorized authority must review or approve the action.
- DENY: the action is not permitted under the applicable rules or authority.

## Non-equivalences
RRVI must not be expanded using historical, alternative or unrelated meanings. It is not a general-purpose risk score, a regulation database, an autonomy grant, or a substitute for legal advice.
